2. Summary

Car Dealer Empire ("the app") is a game that works without an internet connection. It has no user accounts. We run no servers of our own, use no analytics or tracking services of our own, and receive no data through the app that identifies you personally.

The following parties process data:

  • your device (game progress, settings, local notifications),
  • Apple or Google, if you use device backup (iCloud or Android backup),
  • Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) for purchases,
  • Unity Technologies, which provides the built-in purchasing software (Unity IAP),
  • Unity Technologies and ironSource as ad providers for the optional reward videos.

3. Game progress and backup

Your progress is stored on your device. This includes game values, settings, timestamps for offline progress, in-game purchases and the IDs of purchases that were already credited. We have no access to this data.

iOS: The app keeps a copy of your progress in the iCloud key-value store of your Apple ID, so you can load it after reinstalling or on another device with the same Apple ID. This only happens if iCloud is enabled on your device. Apple stores the data in your iCloud account. "Delete progress" in the app also removes this copy.

Android: If you have enabled Android backup in your device settings, Android automatically backs up your progress to your Google account and restores it when you reinstall.

Apple and Google are responsible for these backups under their own privacy policies: Apple, Google. You can turn backup off in your device settings at any time.

Legal basis (EU/UK): Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the game). Storing data on your device is strictly necessary to provide the game (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The data is kept until you delete your progress or uninstall the app. Copies in iCloud or your Google account remain there until deleted (see Support).

4. Notifications

The app can remind you with local notifications, for example when a building is finished or the register is full. These notifications are scheduled on your device only. There is no push server, and no data is sent for this. The app asks for your permission through the operating system first. You can turn notifications off in the game settings or in your system settings at any time.

5. In-app purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive payment details such as your card number, name or address. The store tells the app only which product was bought and gives it a transaction ID. The app uses these to credit the item and to support "Restore purchases". Apple and Google process purchase data as independent controllers: Apple privacy policy, Google privacy policy.

To handle purchases, the app uses Unity In-App Purchasing by Unity Technologies SF, 116 New Montgomery Street, Suite 200, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA (EU contact: Unity Technologies ApS, Niels Hemmingsens Gade 24, 1153 Copenhagen, Denmark; data protection officer: DPO@unity3d.com). According to its privacy disclosure, this software may process purchase history, a user or device identifier, diagnostics and usage data for app functionality and analytics. This data is not used for tracking. Unity's privacy policy.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performing the purchase). Where Unity processes data for analytics and fraud prevention: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is a stable and fraud-resistant purchase process.

6. Reward videos (ads)

You only see ad videos that you start yourself to get an in-game reward. The app never shows ads on its own and has no banners or interstitials. With the No-Ads Pass you get the reward without a video.

We use:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) and Unity Ads: Unity Technologies SF, 116 New Montgomery Street, Suite 200, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA. EU contact: Unity Technologies ApS, Niels Hemmingsens Gade 24, 1153 Copenhagen, Denmark.
  • ironSource Ads: ironSource Mobile Ltd., 121 Menachem Begin Road, Azrieli Sarona Tower, Tel Aviv 6701203, Israel (a Unity affiliate).

No other ad networks are connected. Privacy notices: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (also covers LevelPlay and Unity Ads), Unity and ironSource legal information. Data protection officer: DPO@unity3d.com.

The ad software only starts when you request a video yourself for the first time – not when the app starts, and never with the No-Ads Pass. It then connects to Unity LevelPlay and loads videos so that further videos are ready without waiting. During this and when a video plays, the following data is typically processed:

  • IP address (and, derived from it, an approximate location such as the country),
  • device model, OS, language, app version,
  • on Android, the device advertising ID; on iOS the app does not request the advertising ID (IDFA),
  • ad delivery events (shown, completed, clicked) and data for fraud prevention.

Non-personalized ads only: The app does not ask for tracking permission (no iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt). It explicitly tells the ad providers that there is no consent to personalized ads (GDPR) and that personal data must not be sold or shared (CCPA "do not sell"). The videos are therefore not chosen from a profile built across apps or websites, only from general attributes such as country, language or device type.

Purposes: preloading and delivering videos, granting the reward, limiting how often an ad is repeated, billing advertisers and preventing fraud. The ad providers process some of this data as independent controllers.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because you request the video to get a reward. For billing and fraud prevention: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is funding a free game through voluntary ads. For accessing information on your device: Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

Whether you watch videos is up to you; the game stays fully playable without them. On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID in the device settings.

10. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older, partly because it contains simulated gambling elements (a prize wheel and a slot machine played only with free in-game tickets, no real money). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
